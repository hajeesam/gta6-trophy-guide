    Roadmap to Platinum

    One playthrough is enough. Play the story first, do the one missable thing in Chapter 1, finish the side strands, then clean up free roam — and save the 100% checklist for last. No online trophies exist, and cheats disable trophies (and the save), so keep cheats off for the whole run.

      Save plan — no mission replay

      GTA 6 has no mission replay and no New Game Plus. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter, one more before the final mission, and keep the Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops. [2]

      The 100% checklist (A Long Road)

      The checklist has 35 tasks and only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Anything done during the story still counts. Big counts: 300 of 397 stunts, 15 of 18 meditation spots, 15 of 18 card sets, 200 of 277 vehicles, 70 of 80 challenges. [6]

      Sources & notes

      Every guide in this ledger is written in our own words. Each fact cites one of the sources below by number, beside the step that uses it. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them (as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on all 55). Sources were read 18–19 Nov 2026, around the game’s launch.

        Where sources disagree

        • Meditation count: PowerPyx and GTABase count 18 spots for Positive Mental Attitude; the 100% checklist asks for only 15 of 18. We show both numbers and advise doing all 18. [1][6]
        • Missable or not: PowerPyx flags Positive Mental Attitude as possibly missable; Push Square lists no known missables. Whether the Chapter 1 balcony meditation is strictly required is unconfirmed — do it anyway. [1][7]
        • Platinum time: PowerPyx estimates ~200 hours (difficulty 4/10); Push Square estimates ~250 hours. [1][7]

        What is not in this guide

        • No online trophies: all 55 are single-player. [2][10]
        • No Rockstar logo, artwork, box art or game screenshots are used anywhere in this guide. The look is our own.
        • Hidden trophies: 28 of 55 are hidden on PSN until earned — 12 story, 6 character-quest, 8 one-off secrets, plus Catch Me If You Can and Challenging Tank. [10]
